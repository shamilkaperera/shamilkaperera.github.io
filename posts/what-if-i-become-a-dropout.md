I did not follow the normal path after O/L. I stopped school before A/L and continued it in my own way. Most of my A/L journey was self learning. Somehow, that path worked for me, and I got into University of Peradeniya Engineering. That experience shaped how I learn even today.

Because of that background, I do not fully depend on lectures. Sitting and listening is not enough for me. I cannot really understand something just by hearing it once from a lecturer. It feels like memorizing without meaning. I want to know where the idea begins. I want to understand why the theory exists and how it connects to real life.

![](/images/flux-dev_a_stunning_illustration_of_split_image_of_a_young_person_one_side_studying_engin-0.jpg)

When I take a concept and use it in a real problem, that is when it starts to make sense. If I can apply it and test it, then I understand it better. Just solving past papers without knowing the base does not satisfy me.

There is some psychology behind this. People learn in different ways. Some learn by listening. Others learn by doing. I am more of an active learner. I need to explore, question, and try things on my own. That is why self learning works for me.

But now I am facing a different problem.

I started self learning about content creating. Now I spend more time on it than on my engineering studies. I feel more satisfaction when I create something than when I study theory.

![](/images/flux-dev_a_stunning_illustration_of_student_standing_at_a_crossroads_one_path_with_engine-1.jpg)

Because of this, I am losing time for engineering. Now I have a doubt in my mind. What if I become a drop out? What if this is the main reason?

I feel stuck between two paths. One is stable but slow. The other is exciting but uncertain.

Finally, I am trying my best to complete my degree. At the same time, I want to build a clear path in content creation by the time I graduate.

In the end, self learning gave me everything I have now. Now it is my responsibility to decide where I go next.
