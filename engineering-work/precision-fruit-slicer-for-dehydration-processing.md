  As part of a collaborative engineering team, I developed a specialized fruit slicing machine designed specifically to streamline the fruit dehydration process. In dehydration, slice uniformity is critical for quality control. Our machine was engineered to deliver consistent, precise cuts to ensure even drying and prevent food waste.

  Key Project Phases:

  Requirements Engineering: Utilized QFD (Quality Function Deployment) to systematically translate customer and industry needs into actionable engineering metrics.

  3D CAD Modeling: Fully modeled the machine's components and mechanical assemblies using SolidWorks, ensuring accurate tolerances and smooth operational kinematics.

  Production Optimization: Studied and integrated real-world manufacturing and production processes to ensure the design was not only functional but highly manufacturable and easy to maintain.

  This project was an incredible deep dive into the intersection of food-grade mechanical design, systems engineering, and team collaboration.
